Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

DSME Kids ABC Adventure ("the app") is published by Dawn School of Modern Education (DSME), Johi. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information, device identifiers, usage data or location. It has no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service.

Internet access and permissions

The app requests no Android permissions and does not connect to the internet. All pictures, sounds, the song and the voice output are bundled inside the app or provided by the device's own text-to-speech engine.

Data stored on the device

The app saves the child's progress (current letter, stars, streak) and the sound settings in the app's private storage on the device so that the game can continue where it left off. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled or when a grown-up uses "Reset progress" in the parent area.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children aged 3 to 6. Because it collects no information at all, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children. Settings that could lead outside the child's activity are placed behind a press-and-hold parent gate.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data, no in-app purchases and no links that a child can open.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date changed before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Dawn School of Modern Education (DSME), Johi. Contact details will be published here with the Google Play listing.
