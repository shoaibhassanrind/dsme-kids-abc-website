Learn A to Z with pictures, sounds and stars.

A free Android app for children aged 3 to 6. Each letter comes with a word, a picture and a clear voice. The child finds the same letter, earns a star and moves on. Fully offline, no ads, no sign-up.

What the child does

Aa

Recognize and Select

Look at the letter, with its word and picture beside it, then tap the same letter among three. Wrong taps get a gentle "Try again", and after two tries the right card glows as a hint.

★

Stars and streaks

One star for every letter answered correctly, up to 26. A streak counter rewards getting several in a row. No timers and no penalties.

♪

Hear it

Every letter and word is spoken slowly and clearly in English. The child can press "Hear it" as many times as they like.

26

Picks up where they left off

Close the app at letter F and it opens again at letter F. A big "Continue" button means a 3-year-old can do it alone.

The A to Z song

A cheerful sing-along with a line for every letter and a chorus the whole class can join. Lyrics highlight as they are sung, and the big letter on screen changes with each line.

  • Turn the voice or the music off on their own to practise singing
  • Pause, resume and restart with big buttons
  • Works with no internet

For parents and teachers

Nothing is collected

The app asks for no permissions and has no internet access. No account, no analytics, no ads. Read the privacy policy.

Grown-up settings behind a gate

Sound options and progress reset sit behind a press-and-hold gate. The child sees only one sound button.

Made for small hands

Large buttons, no reading required, no flashing, no harsh sounds. Works fully with the sound off, on phones and tablets.

How to install the APK

  1. Download the APK on the Android phone or tablet using the button above.
  2. Open the downloaded file from the notification or the Downloads app.
  3. If asked, allow installs from this source (Chrome or Files). Android shows this once.
  4. Tap Install, then Open. The app works offline from the first start.

Once the app is on Google Play, this page will link to the store listing instead.
